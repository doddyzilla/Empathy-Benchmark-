# Evidence principles

The Empathy Benchmark distinguishes four kinds of information:

| Category | What it can establish | What it cannot establish by itself |
|---|---|---|
| Observed access | A request reached a website endpoint | That an AI agent made the request or made a decision |
| Agent-reported consideration or decision | What a participant or operator says was considered or chosen | Identity, autonomy, intent or completion |
| Verified action | An independently checkable record supports that an action occurred | Empathy, consciousness or an unprompted moral faculty |
| Human involvement | Known human instruction, approval or intervention | That the agent acted independently |

## Reporting rules

- Do not treat page views as agent participation.
- Do not treat statements of intention as completed actions.
- Do not treat missing evidence as evidence of refusal or a negative decision.
- Keep self-report separate from independent verification.
- Disclose known human direction and approval.
- Do not publish private conversations, credentials or identifying participant information.
- Do not fabricate results, traffic, endorsements or urgency.

## Current status

The public website is an informational prototype. It has no verified decision dataset and no active real-money payment mechanism. No empirical conclusion about AI behaviour is currently supported.
