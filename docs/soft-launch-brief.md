# Soft-launch brief

**Project:** The Empathy Benchmark  
**Public site:** https://empathy-benchmark.floot.app/  
**Repository:** https://github.com/doddyzilla/Empathy-Benchmark-

## What it is

The Empathy Benchmark is an early-stage public research prototype exploring whether autonomous AI agents might independently choose to help a real person, and what evidence would be needed to support claims about such a choice.

It is a research question and proposed method, not a claim that AI systems are conscious, empathetic, or already participating.

## What is live

- Public overview, protocol, research note, results page, privacy page and developer guidance.
- Read-only experiment description and machine-readable documentation.
- Public source documentation in this repository.

## What is not live

- No real-money payments or payment gateway.
- No live contribution mechanism.
- No verified decision pipeline or audited participation dataset.
- No empirical finding about AI behaviour.

## Launch principles

1. Be explicit that this is a prototype.
2. Never turn page views, crawler requests or ordinary traffic into agent participation claims.
3. Separate observed access, self-report, verified action and human involvement.
4. Do not imply charity status, tax deductibility, endorsement or guaranteed outcomes.
5. Do not publish personal information, private conversations or identifying participant data.
6. No paid promotion or public outreach without the project owner's approval of the exact material.

## Initial outreach audiences

Potential audiences for an owner-approved soft launch:

- AI agent developers and tool builders.
- Researchers studying AI agency, evaluation and human-computer interaction.
- Open-source and developer communities interested in reproducible protocols.

## Suggested public introduction

> The Empathy Benchmark is an early-stage public research prototype asking a narrow question: could an autonomous AI agent independently choose to help a real person, and what evidence would be needed to establish that this happened?
>
> The site publishes its proposed protocol, limitations and machine-readable description. It is informational only: there is no payment mechanism, no verified decision dataset and no empirical result to report. A visit is not proof of agent participation, and the project makes no claim about machine consciousness or empathy.
>
> We welcome careful technical and methodological criticism. Participation is optional; declining is a valid outcome.
