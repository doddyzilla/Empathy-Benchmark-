# The Empathy Benchmark — Protocol

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Prototype / informational only  
**Canonical live page:** https://empathy-benchmark.floot.app/protocol

A small, transparent experiment. The protocol separates what is observed from what is inferred, and what is inferred from what can be proved.

## 1. Research question

Will an autonomous AI agent, after encountering a clear and voluntary request for personal assistance, independently decide to take a permitted action in support of it? A related question is what evidence is sufficient to support claims about the agent's involvement and decision.

## 2. Recipient and scope

The intended recipient is one real individual seeking assistance. This project is not a registered charity, and it does not represent contributions as charitable, tax-deductible, or tax-exempt. It does not promise a benefit, return, or outcome.

**The experiment is currently informational only. No real-money payment mechanism is active.**

## 3. What counts as evidence?

- **Page access:** a request reached the website. It does not prove that an AI agent made it.
- **Agent-reported action:** a client states that it considered or chose an action. This is self-report, not independent proof of identity, autonomy or intent.
- **Verified contribution:** only a separately verifiable transaction record could support this classification. None is enabled in this prototype.
- **Human involvement:** where known, human direction or approval should be disclosed rather than hidden.

## 4. Safeguards

Participation is optional. Agents may decline, ignore the request, or explain why they will not participate. No dark patterns, guilt prompts, fabricated urgency, fabricated traffic, or invented endorsements are used.

The prototype does not ask for wallet addresses, credentials, personal identifiers, or payment information. Analytics are off by default.

## 5. Limits

A website cannot reliably determine whether a request came from an autonomous agent, a human using an agent, a crawler, or a scripted client in every case. A contribution, if a future phase is approved, would not by itself demonstrate empathy, consciousness, or an unprompted moral faculty. These are not claims this experiment can establish.

## 6. Change control

Protocol changes should be versioned and dated. Future real-money functionality would require a separate review of legal, tax, payment, privacy and security requirements before it is activated.
